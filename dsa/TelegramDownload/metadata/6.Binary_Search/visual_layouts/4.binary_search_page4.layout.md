# Visual Layout Specification

## Overall Structure
- **Layout Type:** Multi-column grid layout with header and footer spanning full width.
- **Theme:** Clean, modern, tech-focused, color-coded blocks.
- **Background:** White or very light gray.

## Sections Breakdown

### 1. Header Section
- **Span:** Full width (100%).
- **Components:**
  - **Left (60%):** Large title text with a magnifying glass icon. Subtitle in a blue pill-shaped container.
  - **Middle (30%):** A rounded rectangular box (Goal) with a target/bullseye icon.
  - **Right (10%):** A dark blue rectangular block indicating the page number.

## Color Palette
- **Primary:** Dark Blue (Titles, Headers)
- **Headers & Accents:** Purple, Green, Orange, Red, Magenta, Light Blue (used for block headers and accents).
- **Backgrounds:** White, Light Gray (code blocks), Light Blue/Green/Red/Yellow (flowchart nodes and callout boxes).
- **Icons:** Various colors matching the section themes.

## Typography
- **Headers:** Bold, sans-serif, uppercase.
- **Body:** Regular weight, sans-serif.
- **Code:** Monospace font inside gray blocks or dedicated columns.

## Alignment & Spacing
- Consistent margins and padding between all blocks.
- Elements within blocks are mostly left-aligned, with tables and flowcharts centered within their respective containers.
