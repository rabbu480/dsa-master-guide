# Visual Layout Specification: Graph Pattern Guide (Page 18)

## 1. Overall Document Structure
- **Type**: Single-page infographic/cheatsheet.
- **Theme**: Dark blue, light blue, and white with red/orange accents.
- **Layout Grid**: Primarily a multi-column modular grid with full-width sections intersecting it.
- **Typography**: Sans-serif, mostly bold for headers, standard weight for body text.

## 2. Header Section
- **Location**: Top of the page, full width.
- **Elements**:
  - Top-left corner badge: "PAGE 18" in a dark blue rectangular box with white text.
  - Center title: Large, bold, dark blue text "GRAPH PATTERN GUIDE" flanked by an open book icon.
  - Subtitle: "PAGE 18 - GRAPH INTERVIEW BIBLE (ULTIMATE ONE-PAGE REVISION)".
  - Top-right corner box: Pale yellow background with a star icon, text: "THE LAST PAGE. 5 MINUTES BEFORE INTERVIEW? OPEN ME!".
  - Thin separator line with sub-text: "Recognize the pattern. Pick the right tool. Solve with confidence. Crack FAANG!".

## 3. Top Section (3-Column Layout)
- **Structure**: Three distinct columns separated by vertical whitespace.
- **Column 1: 1 PATTERN RECOGNITION FLOW**
  - Header: Dark blue background with white text. Number '1' in a square.
  - Layout: Vertical flowchart.
  - Elements: Circular icons on the left, connected by vertical arrows. Bold title and regular description text on the right of each icon.
  - Footer: "Think → Choose → Code → Test → Optimize" in bold text.
- **Column 2: 2 PROBLEM → SOLUTION DECISION TREE**
  - Header: Dark blue background with white text. Number '2' in a square.
  - Layout: Flowchart diagram.
  - Elements: Rectangular boxes with rounded corners for conditions (light blue outline) and outcomes (red text for algorithms). Arrows indicating "YES" or "NO" flow.
- **Column 3: 3 HIDDEN GRAPH RECOGNITION (THINK LIKE THIS!)**
  - Header: Dark blue background with white text. Number '3' in a square.
  - Layout: Vertical list with icons.
  - Elements: Icon on the left, keyword on top, arrow pointing to the corresponding graph algorithm mapping below it.

## 4. Middle Section (Full-Width Table)
- **Header**: "4 CORE GRAPH ALGORITHMS - WHEN, WHY & HOW" in a dark blue, full-width banner with white text.
- **Structure**: A 6-column table.
- **Headers**: Algorithm, When to Use, Key Idea (AHA), Steps (Quick Process), Core Template (Java), Time, Space.
- **Rows**: Alternating background colors (white and very light gray) separated by thin borders.
- **Content**: Text descriptions, numbered lists (in green circles) for Steps, code blocks for Core Template, and Big-O notation for Time/Space.

## 5. Lower-Middle Section (3-Column Layout)
- **Column 1: 5 GRAPH REPRESENTATIONS**
  - Header: Dark blue banner.
  - Content: Split into two sub-columns "Adjacency List (Preferred)" and "Adjacency Matrix". Shows bullet points and small visual examples (nodes/edges and matrices).
- **Column 2: 6 DIRECTIONS (GRID PROBLEMS)**
  - Header: Dark blue banner.
  - Content: Split into "4 Directions" and "8 Directions". Shows compass/axis diagrams and corresponding code snippets (`int[][] dir4 = ...`, `int[][] dir8 = ...`).
- **Column 3: 7 EDGE TYPES**
  - Header: Dark blue banner.
  - Content: Bulleted list with mappings (e.g., "Directed / Undirected → One way / Two way").

## 6. Bottom Grid Section (4-Column Layout)
- **Column 1: 8 COMPLEXITY SUMMARY**
  - Header: Dark blue banner.
  - Content: A small table summarizing Time and Space complexity for different algorithms.
- **Column 2: 9 COMMON PITFALLS (DON'T LOSE MARKS!)**
  - Header: Dark blue banner.
  - Content: Vertical list of points, each prefixed with a red 'X' mark.
- **Column 3: 10 AHA MOMENTS (MEMORIZE THESE!)**
  - Header: Dark blue banner.
  - Content: Vertical list of points, with small lightbulb icons or arrows mapping concepts.
- **Column 4: 11 INTERVIEW CHECKLIST**
  - Header: Dark blue banner.
  - Content: Vertical checklist with checked box icons.

## 7. Bottom-Most Section (Full-Width Flowchart)
- **Header**: "12 FAANG GRAPH SOLVING PIPELINE" in a dark blue banner.
- **Layout**: Horizontal flowchart.
- **Elements**: 6 steps. Each step has an icon inside a dashed circle, a bold title, and subtext. They are connected by dashed arrows pointing right.

## 8. Footer Block
- **Structure**: Three separate boxes placed side-by-side.
- **Box 1: GOLDEN RULE**
  - Light yellow background, crown icon, bold text.
- **Box 2: REMEMBER**
  - White background, thin border. Two columns of bullet points with star icons.
- **Box 3: YOU'VE GOT THIS!**
  - Light yellow background, bold encouraging text.
